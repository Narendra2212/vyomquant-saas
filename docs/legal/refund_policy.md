# Refund Policy — Aerora Quant Platform Beta

*Effective Date: June 5, 2026*

Aerora Dynamics operates the Aerora Quant Platform. Because the Platform is digital software and runs in a beta simulation phase, we enforce a strict refund policy as detailed below.

## 1. Digital Services & Subscriptions
- Payments for subscription tiers (Pro Tier, Enterprise Tier) and strategy addons (ML/DL model packages) grant immediate access to computing allocation, premium indicator scripts, and telemetry history.
- As such, **all subscription purchases and addon fees are non-refundable**, except as required by law.

## 2. Beta System Downtime Fallbacks
- We do not offer refunds or compensations for service interruptions, API failures, or backtesting delays during the beta phase.
- In extreme cases where payment processing errors lead to double-charging, we will refund the duplicate transaction within 5-10 business days of notification.

## 3. Contact Support
If you believe a billing error has occurred, please open a ticket in the Support Page or contact support directly, providing the Invoice ID and transaction reference.
